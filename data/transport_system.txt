SMART CITY TRANSPORTATION SYSTEM

The city offers multiple transportation options for residents and visitors:

1. PUBLIC BUS SYSTEM
- City Bus Network: 25 routes covering all city districts
- Operating Hours: 5:00 AM - 1:00 AM (Monday-Saturday)
- Sunday Service: 7:00 AM - 11:00 PM
- Night Owl Service: Limited routes 11:00 PM - 5:00 AM

2. FARES AND PASSES
- Single Ride: $2.50
- Day Pass: $7.00 (unlimited rides)
- Monthly Pass: $85.00
- Student/Senior Discount: 50% off with valid ID
- Children under 12: Free with adult

3. BUS STOPS AND SCHEDULES
- Real-time arrival information via City Transit App
- Digital displays at major stops
- Printed schedules available at City Hall and libraries
- Mobile alerts for delays and route changes

4. METRO/LIGHT RAIL
- Red Line: Connects North District to Airport
- Blue Line: East-West corridor through city center
- Green Line: Serves University and Medical District
- Operating Hours: 5:30 AM - 12:30 AM daily
- Trains every 10-15 minutes during peak hours

5. BIKE SHARING
- City Bike Program: 200+ stations across the city
- 24/7 access with mobile app
- First 30 minutes free, then $1 per 30 minutes
- Annual membership: $99.00

6. PARKING
- Street Parking: Free (2-hour limit in residential areas)
- City Garages: $2 per hour, $15 daily maximum
- Metered Parking: $1.50 per hour (8 AM - 6 PM)
- Disabled Parking: Free with valid permit

7. EMERGENCY TRANSPORT
- Medical emergency: Call 911
- Non-emergency medical transport: (555) 555-0100
- Senior citizen transport service available
- ADA accessible vehicles provided
