SMART CITY ID CARD AND PASSPORT SERVICES

The City Clerk's Office handles all identification and passport services:

1. CITY ID CARD
- Purpose: Official city identification for all residents
- Valid for: Accessing city services, age verification, library card
- Cost: 250birr (adults), 150birr (seniors/students), 100birr (children)
- Valid for: 5 years

2. APPLICATION PROCESS
- Visit any City Clerk Office location
- Complete application form (available online)
- Provide proof of residency (utility bill, lease agreement)
- Provide proof of identity (birth certificate, current ID)
- Digital photo taken at office
- Payment by cash, check, or credit card

4. PASSPORT SERVICES
- Full-service passport agency
- Appointment required (call or book online)
- Walk-ins accepted on Wednesdays 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM

5. PASSPORT APPLICATION
- Form DS-11 for first-time applicants
- Proof of U.S. citizenship required
- Government-issued photo ID
- Recent passport photo (or take at office)
- Payment: Check or money order only

6. FEES AND PROCESSING
- Adult Passport Book: $130
- Adult Passport Card: $30
- Minor Passport (under 16): $100
- Processing Time: 6-8 weeks standard
- Expedited Service: Additional $60 (2-3 weeks)

7. RENEWALS AND REPLACEMENTS
- Renew by mail if eligible (Form DS-82)
- Lost or stolen passport: Report immediately
- Replacement fee: $130
- Name change: $110

8. SPECIAL SERVICES
- Emergency travel: Available with proof of travel
- Notary services available
- Certified copies of birth certificates
- Marriage license applications
