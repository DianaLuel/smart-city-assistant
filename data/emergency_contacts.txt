SMART CITY EMERGENCY CONTACTS AND SERVICES

IMPORTANT: In life-threatening emergencies, ALWAYS CALL 911

1. IMMEDIATE EMERGENCY SERVICES
- Police, Fire, Ambulance: 911
- Available 24/7
- Free from any phone (including cell phones)
- Stay on line until told to hang up

2. NON-EMERGENCY NUMBERS
- City Police (Non-Emergency): (555) 555-0110
- Fire Department (Non-Emergency): (555) 555-0120
- Poison Control Center: (800) 222-1222
- Animal Control: (555) 555-0130
- Utilities Emergency: (555) 555-0140

3. MEDICAL EMERGENCIES
- Life-threatening: Call 911 immediately
- Poisoning: Call Poison Control (800) 222-1222
- Mental Health Crisis: (555) 555-0150 (24/7 hotline)
- Nurse Advice Line: (555) 555-0160 (8 AM - 8 PM)

4. NATURAL DISASTERS
- Emergency Management Office: (555) 555-0170
- Severe Weather Alerts: Sign up at www.city.gov/alerts
- Flood Emergency: (555) 555-0180
- Snow Removal Hotline: (555) 555-0190

5. UTILITY EMERGENCIES
- Power Outage: (555) 555-0200
- Gas Leak: (555) 555-0210 (leave area first, then call)
- Water Main Break: (555) 555-0220
- Sewer Backup: (555) 555-0230

6. COMMUNITY SUPPORT SERVICES
- Elder Care Services: (555) 555-0240
- Child Protective Services: (555) 555-0250
- Homeless Services: (555) 555-0260
- Domestic Violence Hotline: (555) 555-0270 (24/7)

7. AFTER-HOURS SERVICES
- City Hall Emergency Line: (555) 555-0280
- Building Inspections (Emergency): (555) 555-0290
- Traffic Signal Out: (555) 555-0300

8. SAFETY TIPS
- Post emergency numbers near all phones
- Teach children how to call 911
- Keep a first aid kit at home
- Know your address for emergency calls
- Prepare an emergency kit for disasters
- Stay informed via city alert system

  Phone: (555) 555-0320

10. VOLUNTEER EMERGENCY SERVICES
