SMART CITY HOSPITAL SERVICES

Our city provides comprehensive healthcare services across multiple facilities:

1. GENERAL HOSPITALS
- City General Hospital: 24/7 emergency care, specialist consultations, surgery
  Address: 123 Main Street, City Center
  Phone: (555) 123-4567
  Hours: Open 24 hours

- Westside Medical Center: Maternity care, pediatrics, cardiology
  Address: 456 West Avenue
  Phone: (555) 234-5678
  Hours: 6:00 AM - 11:00 PM

2. URGENT CARE CENTERS
- Downtown Urgent Care: Non-life-threatening emergencies, minor injuries
  Address: 789 Central Plaza
  Phone: (555) 345-6789
  Hours: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM

- North District Clinic: Walk-in appointments, vaccinations, physical exams
  Address: 321 North Road
  Phone: (555) 456-7890
  Hours: 7:00 AM - 9:00 PM

3. SPECIALIZED SERVICES
- Emergency Room: Available at all general hospitals 24/7
- Ambulance Service: Dial 911 for medical emergencies
- Mental Health Services: Counseling and psychiatric care available
- Pharmacy Services: Prescription fulfillment at all hospital locations

4. APPOINTMENTS
- Call ahead for non-emergency appointments
- Online booking available at www.cityhealth.gov/book
- Walk-ins accepted at urgent care centers

5. COSTS AND INSURANCE
- Accept all major insurance plans
- Sliding scale fees for uninsured residents
- Free health screenings monthly at community centers
